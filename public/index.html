Current Research

Salmon Migration

Long-range acoustic tagging of Pacific salmon populations as they move between spawning rivers and open ocean feeding grounds.

Deep Sea Monitoring

Remote submersible surveys of trench ecosystems, cataloguing species that rarely surface into observable light.

Ocean Temperature

A distributed buoy network recording surface and subsurface temperature drift across major North Pacific currents.

Marine Ecosystems

Long-term population studies tracking how kelp forests and reef systems respond to shifting ocean conditions.

System Note

During a routine system audit, our engineers discovered inconsistencies in several archived monitoring records. Some data appears to belong to an older research system that is no longer maintained.

One of our salmon tracking stations began sending corrupted telemetry shortly before the audit. The station itself appears to be operating normally, but several files and endpoints on the web server seem to have been left behind by a prior research project.

The research team believes there may be something hidden inside the station's website. If you're curious, poke around — the old archive is still out there somewhere.

About the Station

The North Pacific Marine Research Station has operated remote monitoring equipment across the Pacific basin for over a decade, supporting independent researchers studying migration, temperature drift, and deep ocean ecosystems.
